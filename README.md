A phone's last words as its battery hits 1 percent. It holds 1% for four
hours, fields 312 notifications and one very long voice note from your
mother at 3 a.m., denies it is dying — and then dies mid-sentence.

> I held 1% for four hours, 312 notifications, and one very long voice note from your mother at 3 a.m. I am not dying. I am just—

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes that line, exactly, with no trailing
newline, to `entry.txt` under `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (falling back to
`/output/entry.txt`).
